# Conix One Guard

Conix One Guard is a DNS service that protects you against online ads, trackers, and phishing, no matter what platform and device you use. Easy setting up on your router protects your entire network. It blocks ads and trackers on all devices connected to your home Wi-Fi: Game console, PC, Mobile or Smart TV. Make the Internet safe for everyone at home and at work.
